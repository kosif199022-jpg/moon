# Reference-material policy

The source package was accompanied by IFRS, SOCPA, and exam-practice PDFs. Those documents are not committed to this public repository because they may be licensed or copyrighted and may contain edition-specific requirements.

Production knowledge ingestion must store licensed source files in private object storage and record, at minimum:

- publisher and jurisdiction;
- document title, edition, effective date, and superseded status;
- page or paragraph locator;
- content hash and ingestion version;
- distinction between official text, internal interpretation, and AI-generated inference.

No standards answer may invent a paragraph number or present an inference as official text.
